一杯奶茶 一份人情味 A cup of milk tea a taste of home

友愛冰室係 Salford 嘅香港冰室，由 Salford Hongkongers CIC 同一班義工一齊營運。我哋想喺 Salford 留住香港冰室嘅味道同人情味，畀新來嘅港人、街坊同朋友有個地方坐低、食餐好、傾吓偈。

Yau Oi Cafe is a Hong Kong-style café in Salford, run by Salford Hongkongers CIC and a team of volunteers. We want to keep the taste and warmth of a Hong Kong bing sutt alive here — a place for new arrivals, neighbours and friends to sit down, eat well and chat.

我哋嘅理念What we stand for

香港味道Taste of Hong Kong
港式早餐、通粉、三文治、即日焗嘅菠蘿包同蛋撻，配一杯絲襪奶茶。
Hong Kong breakfasts, macaroni soup, sandwiches, freshly baked pineapple buns and egg tarts, with a proper milk tea.
社區聚腳點A community meeting place
唔止係食飯，係一個識新朋友、互相照應嘅地方。
More than a meal — a place to meet people and look out for each other.
義工營運Run by volunteers
由 Salford Hongkongers CIC 嘅義工落手落腳打理，收益用返喺營運社區中心同舉辦社區活動。
Run hands-on by Salford Hongkongers CIC volunteers. What we earn goes back into running the community centre and holding community events.

點樣落單How to order

堂食Eat in

坐低之後用手機 scan 枱上嘅 QR code 落單，落單後即到櫃台付款（現金或者卡）。

Sit down, scan the QR code on your table to order, then pay at the counter straight away (cash or card).

外賣Takeaway

scan 門口嘅外賣 QR code 落單，落單後即到櫃台付款。

Scan the takeaway QR code at the entrance then pay at the counter straight away.

人多要等位Busy?

用網上排隊取號，輪到你手機會響。

Take a number with the online queue — your phone alerts you when it's your turn.

食物過敏？ 每樣餐點嘅過敏原都寫喺餐牌上。落單前請話俾員工知，或者喺訂單「過敏備註」寫低。

Food allergy? Allergens are listed for every item on the menu. Please tell staff before ordering, or add an allergy note to your order.
