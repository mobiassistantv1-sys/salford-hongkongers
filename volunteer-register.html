凡每年累積義工服務滿 10 小時，我們樂意為你簽發「義工服務證明」。由於證明需載明你的法定全名，如你日後有意索取，請填寫全名；若暫時無此需要，只填暱稱即可。
We're happy to issue a Volunteer Service Certificate to anyone who completes 10+ hours in a year. As it must show your legal name, please provide your full name if you may want one — otherwise a nickname is enough.

你的個人資料只用於義工聯絡及服務安排，最長保存 5 年；若你辭任義工，我們會於一年內刪除。你可隨時電郵 salfordhongkongers@gmail.com 要求查閱、更正或刪除。
Your details are used only to contact you and arrange volunteering. We keep them for up to 5 years and delete them within one year of you stepping down. Email us anytime to access, correct or erase your data.
