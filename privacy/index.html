私隱政策

2026 年版 · 最後更新：2026 年 10 月

呢份政策講解 Salford Hongkongers CIC（「SHK」、「我哋」）點樣收集、使用同保存你嘅個人資料，包括網站、活動、義工同友愛冰室。我哋按照 UK GDPR 同 2018 年資料保護法（Data Protection Act 2018）處理你嘅資料。

1. 我哋係邊個

資料控制者：Salford Hongkongers CIC（社區利益公司，公司編號 13877472，註冊地址 C/O MTM, 3rd Floor, 82 King Street, Manchester M2 4WQ）。

私隱查詢、要求睇／改／刪資料：salfordhongkongers@gmail.com

2. 我哋收集咩資料、點解要、法律基礎

情況資料用途法律基礎
義工帳戶同打卡名、英文名（選填）、WhatsApp 號碼、電郵（選填）、每次活動嘅上落班時間安排義工工作、聯絡你、計算義工時數、每年義工頒獎禮、向資助機構報告義工總時數正當利益（管理義工）
冰室員工帳戶同上，另加你喺後台做過嘅操作（例如俾折扣、收款）冰室運作、對數、防止錯誤同濫用正當利益；會計記錄屬法律責任
活動報名名、電話、電郵、郵區（例如 M6）、你自願填寫嘅備註安排活動、通知你活動消息、向資助機構報告參加人數同地區（只用總數）正當利益
特別需要、飲食限制、食物過敏你自願話俾我哋知嘅健康相關資料只用嚟安排嗰次活動或者準備你嗰張單你嘅明確同意（你可以唔填）
冰室排隊名、電話、人數叫你入座正當利益
冰室 QR 落單枱號、訂單、你自願填寫嘅名同過敏備註準備食物、收款、對數履行你嘅訂單；過敏備註見上
查詢、意見、合作名、電郵、電話（選填）、你寫嘅內容回覆你正當利益
活動相片同影片你嘅樣貌記錄活動、喺社交媒體同資助報告宣傳正當利益；18 歲以下會先問家長

唔想被影相，請喺活動開始前話俾工作人員知。相片已經出咗，想我哋撤下，請電郵我哋。

3. 付款

冰室刷卡由 Square 處理，捐款由 PayPal 處理。我哋唔會見到或者保存你嘅完整卡號。佢哋係獨立嘅資料控制者，請參閱佢哋嘅私隱政策。

4. 我哋會唔會分享你嘅資料

  • 我哋唔會出售你嘅資料，亦唔會俾其他機構做推廣。
  • 我哋用以下服務供應商代我哋存放同處理資料：Google（Firebase、Google Sheets、Gmail、Google Forms）、GitHub（網站寄存）、Square、PayPal。
  • 資助機構：只會收到總數（例如義工總時數、參加人數、郵區分佈），唔會列出你個名，除非事先問過你。
  • 義工頒獎禮會公佈得獎義工嘅名同時數。唔想被公佈，請話我哋知。
  • 法律要求嘅時候（例如警方或者法院依法要求、保護兒童同弱勢人士）。

5. 資料存放喺邊

義工、冰室同活動資料存放喺 Google 嘅伺服器。部分資料可能喺英國以外（包括歐洲經濟區同美國）處理；Google 用英國法例認可嘅保障措施保護呢啲資料。

6. 我哋保存幾耐

  • 義工資料：以下任何一樣發生就刪除：(1) 你要求刪除；(2) 你退出義工之後兩年內；(3) 你參與嘅義工計劃完成之後兩年。如果資助協議要求保存更長時間，我哋只會保留嗰份協議需要嘅記錄，並盡量用唔具名嘅總數代替。
  • 收款同會計記錄：按法例要求保存 6 年。
  • 冰室訂單上嘅名、過敏備註同其他備註：30 日後自動刪除；訂單內容同收款記錄按會計要求保留。
  • 冰室排隊：每日自動刪除前一日嘅記錄。
  • 活動報名：活動完咗之後兩年內刪除；報告用嘅總數會保留。
  • 查詢同意見：處理完之後兩年內刪除。

7. 你嘅權利

你有權：

  • 要求睇我哋保存咗你咩資料，同埋攞一份副本
  • 更正錯誤嘅資料
  • 要求刪除你嘅資料
  • 要求我哋暫停使用你嘅資料
  • 反對我哋基於正當利益使用你嘅資料
  • 隨時撤回你俾過嘅同意（例如過敏備註、相片）
  • 攞返你嘅資料，轉俾其他機構

請電郵 salfordhongkongers@gmail.com。我哋會喺一個月內回覆，一般唔收費。為咗保護你，我哋可能要先核實你嘅身份。

8. 我哋點樣保護你嘅資料

  • 帳戶要用個人密碼登入；唔同人只可以睇到工作需要嘅資料。
  • 義工個人資料只有 SHK 指定負責人睇到。
  • 客人落單頁面同後台完全分開。

9. 兒童

18 歲以下人士參加活動或者做義工，要先由家長或者監護人簽署同意書。18 歲以下義工嘅帳戶，我哋只會喺收到簽好嘅同意書之後先批核。同意書同義工資料一樣按第 6 條保存。

10. 投訴

有任何疑問或者投訴，請先電郵 salfordhongkongers@gmail.com。你亦有權向英國資訊專員辦公室（ICO）投訴：ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint，電話 0303 123 1113。

11. 更新

我哋會不時更新呢份政策，最新版本會放喺呢一頁。

Privacy Policy

2026 edition · Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains how Salford Hongkongers CIC ("SHK", "we") collects, uses and keeps your personal data across our website, events, volunteering and Yau Oi Cafe. We handle your data under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Who we are

Data controller: Salford Hongkongers CIC, a community interest company (company number 13877472, registered office C/O MTM, 3rd Floor, 82 King Street, Manchester M2 4WQ).

Privacy questions and requests to see, correct or delete your data: salfordhongkongers@gmail.com

3. Payments

Card payments at the cafe are handled by Square and donations by PayPal. We never see or store your full card number. They are independent data controllers; please see their privacy policies.

4. Who we share your data with

  • We never sell your data or give it to other organisations for marketing.
  • These service providers store and process data on our behalf: Google (Firebase, Google Sheets, Gmail, Google Forms), GitHub (website hosting), Square and PayPal.
  • Funders receive totals only (e.g. total volunteer hours, attendance, postcode districts), never your name unless we ask you first.
  • At our annual volunteer awards we announce award winners' names and hours. Tell us if you'd rather not be named.
  • When the law requires it (e.g. a lawful request from the police or a court, or to protect children and vulnerable people).

5. Where your data is stored

Volunteer, cafe and event data is stored on Google's servers. Some data may be processed outside the UK (including the EEA and the US); Google protects it with safeguards recognised under UK law.

6. How long we keep it

  • Volunteer data: deleted when any of these happens: (1) you ask us to delete it; (2) within two years after you stop volunteering; (3) two years after the volunteer programme you took part in ends. If a funding agreement requires us to keep records for longer, we keep only what that agreement needs and use anonymous totals wherever we can.
  • Payment and accounting records: 6 years, as the law requires.
  • Names, allergy notes and other notes on cafe orders: deleted automatically after 30 days; the items ordered and payment records are kept for the accounts.
  • Cafe queue: previous days' entries are deleted automatically every night.
  • Event sign-ups: deleted within two years after the event; totals used in reports are kept.
  • Enquiries and feedback: deleted within two years after we've dealt with them.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • see what data we hold about you and get a copy
  • correct data that is wrong
  • ask us to delete your data
  • ask us to stop using your data for a while
  • object to our use of your data based on legitimate interests
  • withdraw any consent you gave at any time (e.g. allergy notes, photos)
  • get your data back to give to another organisation

Email salfordhongkongers@gmail.com. We reply within one month and normally don't charge. To protect you, we may need to confirm your identity first.

8. How we protect your data

  • Accounts use personal passwords, and each person can see only what their role needs.
  • Volunteers' personal details are visible only to designated SHK organisers.
  • The customer ordering pages are completely separate from the back office.

9. Children

Under-18s need a parent or guardian to sign a consent form before joining activities or volunteering. We approve an under-18 volunteer's account only after we receive the signed form. Consent forms are kept in the same way as volunteer data (section 6).

10. Complaints

If you have a question or complaint, please email salfordhongkongers@gmail.com first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, phone 0303 123 1113.

11. Changes

We update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page.
